CU Boulder MENV Capstone · SVLH Water Conservancy District

How much water does St. Vrain Creek need?

Colorado water law protects whoever claimed the water first — not the creek itself. We surveyed St. Vrain Creek reach by reach to find the minimum flow each stretch needs to stay healthy, and turned it into numbers the District and its partners can act on.

Shadia Nagati & Jessica Reid
